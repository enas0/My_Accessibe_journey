About

Your Voice Shapes the Journey

At My Accessible Journey, we work to make travel easier and more inclusive for everyone.

To keep improving, we rely on your real experiences. Here, you can share your trip stories — the places you visited, what was accessible, and what challenges you faced. Your feedback helps us refine our services and understand what travelers truly need.

We also use your insights to report accessibility issues to the Ministry of Tourism, supporting efforts to improve and prepare destinations for all. Together, we can build a better travel experience for every traveler.
