About Trip

A full-day visit to Petra: enter through the Siq, admire the Treasury, walk the Roman Street, climb to the Monastery, and enjoy panoramic viewpoints. Suitable for moderate fitness; includes a licensed guide and bottled water.

Who is this for?
History lovers, photographers, active travellers. Not recommended for people with severe mobility issues.
