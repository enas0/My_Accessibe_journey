About Trip

Half-day retreat: transport from Amman, resort entrance, floating session, mineral mud application and optional spa access. Family-friendly and suitable for most fitness levels.

Who is this for?
Relaxation seekers, couples, families and wellness travelers. Not suitable for open wounds or sensitive skin — consult your doctor if unsure.
