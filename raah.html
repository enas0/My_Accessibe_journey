360 Tours — Accessibility Friendly

Explore archaeological and natural sites in Jordan with audio descriptions, keyboard controls and an accessible experience.

About the featured places

These virtual tours are designed to be accessible for all users, including people with mobility, visual or hearing impairments. Each site includes a full audio description and you can explore three famous sites: Petra, Jerash and Shobak. The tours provide a realistic sense of being on-site, with a clear and simple interface.
